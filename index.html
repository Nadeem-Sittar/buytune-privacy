Privacy Policy — Buytune

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Buytune is a desktop point-of-sale application for Windows, published by Muhammad Nadeem. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. What the app is

Buytune runs entirely on your computer. It is designed so that your business data never leaves your machine.

2. What we store

The app stores business data in a local database on your computer, including:

  • Your shop name, address, phone number and email
  • Owner and staff names, roles and PIN hashes (PINs are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text)
  • Product names, prices, stock records and supplier details
  • Customer and supplier contact details you enter (names, phone numbers)
  • Sales, refunds, claims, expenses and report history

3. What we do not do

Buytune in offline mode does not:

  • Collect personal data for us
  • Send your data to our servers or to any third party
  • Include analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • Require an account or internet connection

4. Internet connectivity

The core app works without internet. If you choose to use future optional online features (cloud sync or backup), those features will transmit your data over HTTPS to operate, and this policy will be updated to describe them before they ship. The app may check for updates over HTTPS; this check does not send your business data. Backups you create are your responsibility; cloud backups require an internet connection and local backups are tied to your chosen media folder. Buytune is not responsible for data loss if backups are not maintained.

5. Media files

Images you attach (store logo, product photos, invoice images) are stored in the media folder you select on your computer, not in the cloud.

6. Data deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes the program; to delete your business data as well, delete the Buytune data folder (%APPDATA%\Buytune) and your selected media folder. Contact us if you need help locating them.

7. Children

Buytune is a business tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
